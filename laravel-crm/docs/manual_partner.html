Who this guide is for

External referral partners who send business to the developer and earn commission.

Your access: you only ever see My Portal — your own referral link, your own leads, your own bookings and your own commission. You cannot see other partners' data or the company's internal leads. This keeps your business private and secure.

Logging in

  1. Open the CRM link the developer shared with you.
  2. Type your email (your login ID) and password.
  3. Click Sign in. If it's your first time, set a new password when asked.
  4. You land directly on My Portal.

Your home screen — My Portal

TASK 1 — Share your referral link

  1. At the top of the portal, find "Your referral link".
  2. Click Copy.
  3. Paste and share it with prospects (WhatsApp, email, social media).
  4. When someone comes in through your link, that lead is automatically credited to you — so is any commission if they book.
Why this matters: the link is how the system knows a lead and its commission belong to you. Always share your own link.

TASK 2 — Brand your website chat widget

  1. Find the "Chat widget branding" box.
  2. Type a Title (e.g. "Prime Realty Chat").
  3. Pick an Accent colour.
  4. Type a friendly Greeting message (e.g. "Namaste! How can we help?").
  5. Click Save branding.
  6. Copy the embed snippet shown below and paste it into your own website — leads it captures are auto-credited to you.

TASK 3 — Track your leads, bookings and commission

  1. Look at the four summary cards: My Leads, My Bookings, Earned and Pending.
  2. Scroll to My Bookings to see each booking, the customer, the commission amount and its status (paid / pending).
  3. Earned is commission already paid to you; Pending is what's still to come.
Commission status: "paid" means the developer has released your commission; "pending" means it's approved and on the way, or awaiting the customer's payment milestone.

Quick answers

QuestionAnswer
Why can't I see other leads?By design — partners only see their own referrals, keeping everyone's data private.
How do I make sure I get credit?Always share your own referral link or your branded widget.
When do I get paid?Commission moves from Pending to Earned once the developer releases it.
