Your home screen

Legal work in the CRM is booking-driven: you act on customers once their deal is confirmed and allotment is triggered. You'll mostly use Bookings and the individual customer records.

How your menu looks: your side menu shows Overview, Sales (to view leads), Deals & Finance (Bookings, Collections, Demand Letters) and the WhatsApp Inbox. You work legal actions inside the relevant booking / customer record.

TASK 1 — Find the bookings that are ready for you

  1. Open Deals & Finance → Bookings.
  2. Look for bookings marked Confirmed — these are live deals.
  3. Click a booking to open its full record.
  4. Check whether allotment has been triggered (this happens once the customer has paid 10%).

TASK 2 — Prepare and manage the agreement

  1. Open the confirmed booking (Task 1).
  2. Find the Agreement / Documents section of the record.
  3. Click Prepare agreement to start the agreement for that customer.
  4. Check the customer and unit details are correct before you proceed.
  5. Generate the agreement document.
  6. Move the agreement through its stages (Draft → Sent → Signed) as it progresses.
  7. Send it for signature — e-sign is currently a demo/mock step until an e-sign provider is connected.
  8. Once signed, mark the agreement complete on the record.
Note: electronic signature is mocked today. The agreement lifecycle (prepare, send, mark signed) works, but real e-signature activates only after an e-sign provider is connected in Integrations.

TASK 3 — Review a customer's leads & history

  1. Open Sales → Leads (you have view access).
  2. Search for the customer by name, phone or email.
  3. Open the record to read the full activity trail, notes and documents.
  4. Use this to confirm details before finalising an agreement.

TASK 4 — Coordinate with Accounts on allotment

  1. Allotment is triggered automatically once the customer's collections reach 10%.
  2. Check the booking's collection status under Deals & Finance → Collections.
  3. When allotment is triggered, begin the agreement (Task 2).
  4. Keep the Accounts team informed of the agreement's progress.
