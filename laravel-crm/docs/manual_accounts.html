Your home screen

Your day mostly lives in three screens: Bookings, Collections and Demand Letters (all under Deals & Finance).

TASK 1 — Review bookings and token payments

  1. Open Deals & Finance → Bookings.
  2. Read the top summary: Total deal value, Confirmed bookings and Token collected.
  3. Scan the table — each row shows the booking reference, customer, deal value, token and status (Confirmed, Form sent, Cancelled).
  4. Click a booking to open its full record.
  5. Check that the token shows as paid for confirmed deals.

TASK 2 — Record and verify a payment

  1. Open the booking (Task 1) or the customer's record.
  2. Click Add payment / Record receipt.
  3. Enter the amount, date and mode (cheque, transfer, UPI…).
  4. Enter the reference number (transaction/UTR/cheque no.).
  5. Click Save.
  6. Open the payment and click Verify / Reconcile once the money is confirmed in the bank.
  7. A receipt is generated for the customer.
Support staff: you can view all of this but the Verify and edit actions are done by the Accounts Head.

TASK 3 — Track collections and ageing

  1. Open Deals & Finance → Collections.
  2. Read the three big numbers: Collected, Scheduled and Outstanding.
  3. Look at the Ageing of receivables row — Current, 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days.
  4. Scroll to Overdue milestones to see exactly which bookings owe money and by how long.
  5. Prioritise the oldest overdue amounts first.
Why ageing matters: anything drifting into 60+ days needs a call or a demand letter. Use this screen to decide who to chase today.

TASK 4 — Raise and manage demand letters

  1. Open Deals & Finance → Demand Letters.
  2. Read the top summary: Total due, Escalated and Settled.
  3. To raise a new one, click New demand letter (or the action on an overdue milestone).
  4. Pick the booking / milestone the demand is for.
  5. Check the amount and any interest the system has added for the delay.
  6. The letter gets a unique serial number automatically.
  7. Click Generate / Send.
  8. When the customer pays, open the letter and mark it Settled.
Escalation: letters that stay unpaid move to Escalated so nothing is forgotten.

TASK 5 — Allotment at 10% collection

  1. The CRM tracks how much a customer has paid against their deal.
  2. Once collections reach 10%, the system triggers allotment for that booking.
  3. Coordinate with the Legal team, who take the agreement forward from there.
