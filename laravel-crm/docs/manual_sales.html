Your home screen — the Sales Cockpit

Start every day here. Your dashboard is a live cockpit built for selling:

  • Top Prospects — your best leads (highest score and most recently engaged), each with a one-line AI summary of the latest WhatsApp messages and notes, so you know exactly where a customer stands before you call. Click one to open the full lead.
  • Lead Flow Journey — a railway-style signal map showing how many of your leads sit at each stage, from New Lead through to Won.
  • Calendar — this week's tasks and site visits; tap any day to see what's on for it.
  • KPIs & charts — new leads this week, hot leads, open/overdue tasks and conversions, plus a pipeline funnel, a 14-day trend and your top lead sources.

A BDE sees their own leads; a BDM or Sales Head sees the whole team's book. Then check your Call List and SLA Board so nothing is missed. Press Ctrl/Cmd + K anytime to find a lead by name.

TASK 1 — Add a new lead by hand

  1. Open Sales → Leads.
  2. Click Add lead (top of the page).
  3. Enter the person's name.
  4. Enter their phone number and/or email.
  5. Choose the source (Walk-in, Website, Referral…).
  6. Add their need, budget and preferred location if known.
  7. Click Save.
  8. If someone with the same phone/email already exists, the CRM warns you it's a duplicate — open the existing lead instead of making a second one.

TASK 2 — Find and open a lead

  1. Open Sales → Leads.
  2. Type a name, phone or email in the search box, or use the Stage / Temp / Source dropdown filters.
  3. Click the lead's row to open their detail drawer.
  4. Inside you'll see their profile, activity history, calls, WhatsApp chat and the Journey tab.
The Journey tab shows a live "train track" of where the lead is — which steps are done, which is happening now and what's waiting. It updates on its own.

TASK 3 — Log a call and set the next follow-up

  1. Open the lead (Task 2).
  2. Click Log call (or the phone/activity action).
  3. Choose the outcome (Connected, No answer, Busy…).
  4. Type a short note of what was discussed.
  5. Set the next follow-up date/time so it appears on your task list.
  6. Click Save.

TASK 4 — Work your daily Call List

  1. Open Sales → Call List. It is already sorted — Hot and high-score leads first.
  2. Work down the list top to bottom.
  3. Click a row to open the lead, then log the call (Task 3).
  4. Check the Last contact column so you don't call the same person twice in a day.

TASK 5 — Move a lead along the Pipeline

  1. Open Sales → Pipeline.
  2. Find the lead's card in its current column.
  3. Click and hold the card, then drag it to the next stage column and release.
  4. A short confirmation (toast) appears when the stage is updated.
  5. Click a card to open the full lead if you need to add notes.
Temperature dots: each card shows Hot/Warm/Cold so you can prioritise at a glance.

TASK 6 — Schedule a site visit

  1. Open Sales → Site Visits (or open the lead and choose Schedule visit).
  2. Pick the lead, project, and date & time.
  3. Click Save — the customer gets a reminder (once messaging is live).
  4. On visit day, use check-in when you arrive and check-out when done (captures location).
  5. After the visit, record the outcome (Interested, Not interested, Reschedule) within 2 hours.
  6. If the customer didn't turn up, mark it a no-show.

TASK 7 — Check unit availability (Inventory)

  1. Open Sales → Inventory.
  2. Read the top summary: Available, Held, Booked, Sold and % absorbed.
  3. Use the legend colours to read the unit map: outline = available, amber = held, lime = booked, black = sold.
  4. Click a unit to see or (if allowed) update its details.

TASK 8 — Stay on top of your tasks and SLAs

  1. Open Overview → SLA Board each morning to spot anything overdue or at risk.
  2. Open Overview → Tasks to see your personal to-do list.
  3. Click any item to open the related lead and act on it.

TASK 9 — Message a customer on WhatsApp

  1. Open Messaging → WhatsApp Inbox.
  2. Pick a conversation from the left list (use All / Mine / Unread to filter).
  3. Read the thread in the middle; the customer's lead details show on the right.
  4. Type your reply at the bottom and send. Use a canned reply to insert a ready-made message.
  5. Click Open full lead on the right to jump to the lead's record.
Note: real sending/receiving works once your Admin connects the company WhatsApp account under Integrations. Until then the inbox runs in demo mode.

TASK 10 — Request or approve a discount

If you are a BDE / BDM (requesting)

  1. Open the lead's cost sheet / proposal.
  2. Enter the discount you want to offer.
  3. If it's above 5% (or 10%), the system sends it for approval automatically.

If you are the Sales Head (approving)

  1. Open Deals & Finance → Discount Approvals.
  2. Review each request — discount %, amount and the band (>5% / >10%).
  3. Click Approve, Counter (offer a different figure) or Reject.

The lead journey (how a deal flows)

  1. Lead arrives → duplicate check.
  2. Verify & make first contact on time.
  3. Score & temperature (Hot / Warm / Cold).
  4. Nurture with the right follow-up sequence.
  5. Schedule and complete a site visit.
  6. Build proposal / cost sheet; get discount approval if needed.
  7. Win the deal → booking is created and locked.
