# Real Estate CRM — User Manual

*A plain-English guide for everyone who uses the system. No technical background needed.*

---

## 1. What is this CRM?

This CRM is the single place where your real estate business manages **everything about a customer** — from the first enquiry all the way to booking, payments, and handing over the property.

Think of it as your company's shared "control room". Sales, Accounts, Legal, CRM support, management, and channel partners all work in the same system, but each person only sees the parts that are relevant to their job.

**In one line:** it captures leads, helps your team follow up on time, tracks site visits and bookings, manages payments and documents, sends WhatsApp and email messages, and shows management the full picture — all in one tool.

---

## 2. Getting started (first time)

### 2.1 Logging in
1. Open the CRM link in your web browser.
2. Enter your **email** (this is your login ID) and **password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.

### 2.2 Your first login
- The very first time, your temporary password is usually your **phone number** (your admin will confirm).
- The system will ask you to **create a new password**. Choose something only you know.
- After that, you'll land on your **home screen**, which is tailored to your role.

### 2.3 Forgot where things are?
- Press **Ctrl + K** (or **Cmd + K** on Mac) anywhere in the app to open the **quick search**. You can search for a lead by name or jump to any page instantly.
- Your **name and photo** at the bottom-left open your **Account Settings** (profile, notifications, password).

## 3. The first-time company setup (for the Admin only)

When the business first switches on the CRM, the **Admin** is guided through a short **setup wizard**:
1. Set up the company basics.
2. Add the first projects (and their phases and plots/units).
3. Add team members and assign their roles.

While the wizard is running, the side menu stays hidden so you can focus. Once you finish (or choose to skip to the dashboard), the full menu appears.

You can always **restart the setup** later from the dashboard if you want to redo the demo.

## 4. Who sees what — roles at a glance

The CRM has **12 roles**. Each role only sees the menus and actions it needs. Here is what each person can expect.

| Role | What they mainly do | What they can see |
|------|---------------------|-------------------|
| **Super Admin** | Owns and controls the whole system | Everything |
| **Process Admin** | Runs day-to-day setup: users, projects, automations, integrations | Setup, users, projects, integrations, all leads (view) |
| **Sales Head** | Leads the sales team, approves discounts | All leads, pipeline, visits, projects, discount approvals |
| **BDM** (Business Development Manager) | Manages a group of sales executives | Their leads, pipeline, visits |
| **BDE** (Business Development Executive) | Front-line sales, works leads daily | Their leads, pipeline, call list, visits |
| **Accounts Head** | Oversees money: payments, collections | Accounts, collections, post-sales, leads (view) |
| **Accounts Support** | Helps with accounts tasks (no delete/approve) | Accounts (view), leads (view) |
| **Legal Head** | Manages agreements and legal documents | Legal module, leads (view) |
| **Legal Support** | Assists legal work (limited) | Legal (view), leads (view) |
| **CRM Head** | Manages customer relationship / post-sales | CRM module, post-sales, leads (view + edit) |
| **CRM Support** | Assists CRM tasks (limited) | CRM (view), leads (view) |
| **Channel Partner** | External referral partner | Only their own partner portal |

**Rule of thumb:** *Heads* can create, edit, approve and delete within their department. *Support* staff can view and help, but cannot delete or approve. **Channel Partners** are kept completely separate — they only ever see their own referrals, bookings and commissions.

## 5. The main areas of the CRM (menu guide)

Below is every section in simple terms, grouped exactly as they appear in the side menu.

### 5.1 Overview
- **Dashboard** — your personalised home screen with key numbers (total leads, hot/warm/cold split, pipeline funnel, recent activity).
- **SLA Board** — a "heat board" showing what is due, overdue, or at risk, so nothing slips.
- **Tasks** — your personal to-do list (follow-ups, calls, reminders).

### 5.2 Sales
- **Leads** — the full list of enquiries. Open any lead to see their full story, notes, calls and WhatsApp chat.
- **Pipeline** — a drag-and-drop board. Move a lead across stages (New → Contacted → Qualified → Booked, etc.) by dragging their card.
- **Call List** — the prioritised list of people to call today.
- **Site Visits** — schedule visits, send reminders, record whether the customer showed up, and capture the visit outcome (with location check-in/check-out).
- **Inventory** — a visual map of every project's plots/units showing what is Available, Held, Booked or Sold.
- **Import** — bring in leads in bulk from a spreadsheet (CSV).

### 5.3 Deals & Finance
- **Bookings** — confirmed deals, token amounts and booking records.
- **Collections** — money received vs. scheduled vs. outstanding, plus ageing of overdue amounts.
- **Demand Letters** — automatically raise payment demand letters with serial numbers and interest on delays.
- **Discount Approvals** *(Sales Head)* — approve or reject discount requests above set limits.

### 5.4 Messaging
- **WhatsApp Inbox** — a live, three-panel chat screen: conversation list, the chat itself, and the customer's details on the right. Reply, assign chats, add notes and tags.
- **WA Broadcasts** — send a WhatsApp message to many customers at once.
- **WA Auto-Replies** — set up automatic replies to common questions.
- **WA Templates** — manage approved WhatsApp message templates.
- **WA Canned Replies** — save ready-made replies your team can reuse in one click.
- **WA Analytics** — response times, unread counts and per-agent performance.
- **Email Templates** — design reusable email designs.
- **Email Campaigns** — send bulk emails, schedule them, and track opens and clicks.
- **Chat Widget** — the chat box you embed on your website to capture leads automatically.

### 5.5 Automation & Setup
- **Flow Builder** — a visual, drag-and-connect tool to build "if this, then that" journeys (e.g. *when a new hot lead arrives → send a WhatsApp → wait 1 day → create a follow-up task*). No coding needed.
- **Automations** — the running rules that fire automatically.
- **Lead Scoring** — set how leads earn points to become Hot, Warm or Cold.
- **Templates** — reusable message templates.
- **Payment Plans** — define installment plans for projects.

### 5.6 Partners
- **Channel Partners** — manage your external referral partners.
- **Commissions** — track and approve the commission partners earn.

### 5.7 Administration
- **Users & Roles** — add team members and assign their role.
- **Roles & Access** — fine-tune exactly what each role can do (turn individual permissions on/off).
- **Integrations** — connect your own accounts (WhatsApp, Email, Mcube calling) — see Section 7.
- **Preview Roles** *(Super Admin)* — safely "see the app as" another role to check what they can access.
- **System Health** — a quick status check of the system.
- **Audit Log** — a record of who did what and when.

### 5.8 Partner Portal (Channel Partners only)
- **My Portal** — partners see only their own referred leads, bookings and commission earnings. Nothing else.

## 6. How leads flow through the CRM (the customer journey)

This is the heart of the CRM. Here is the typical journey in plain steps:

1. **Lead arrives** — from your website, a WhatsApp message, a manual entry, a bulk import, or a partner referral.
2. **Duplicate check** — the system blocks exact duplicates and flags similar-looking ones so you don't chase the same person twice.
3. **Verification & first contact** — the lead is verified and assigned; the team must make first contact within the set time.
4. **Scoring & temperature** — the lead is scored and marked **Hot (70+)**, **Warm (40–69)** or **Cold (below 40)**.
5. **Nurturing** — Hot, Warm and Cold leads each get their own follow-up sequence (calls, WhatsApp, email).
6. **Site visit** — schedule the visit, remind the customer, and log what happened.
7. **Handover** — a serious lead is handed to the right team; first contact must happen within 24 hours.
8. **Proposal & discount** — build cost sheets and payment plans; large discounts go for approval.
9. **Booking** — the deal is won, a booking is created, and the record is locked.
10. **Payments & receipts** — payments are recorded, verified and reconciled; welcome documents are shared.
11. **Allotment & agreement** — once 10% is collected, allotment is triggered and the agreement process begins.
12. **Collections & demand letters** — the system tracks milestones, sends demand letters and applies interest on late payments.

**Live Journey view:** open any lead and click the **Journey** tab to see a live "train track" of exactly where they are — which steps are done, which is current, and what's waiting. It updates automatically.

## 7. Connecting your own accounts (Integrations Hub)

*Available to Admin and Process Admin only.*

The CRM works out-of-the-box in a **demo/mock mode** so you can try everything safely. When you're ready to go live with real messages, emails and calls, you connect your **own accounts** in **Administration → Integrations**.

You'll see three connection cards:

1. **Meta WhatsApp Cloud** — for real WhatsApp sending/receiving.
2. **Google Workspace Email** — for real email sending (using a dedicated mailbox + App Password).
3. **Mcube** — for calling/telephony.

### How to connect (general steps)
1. Go to **Administration → Integrations**.
2. Click the card you want to set up.
3. Fill in the details in the form (each field is labelled; required fields are marked with `*`).
4. Click **Test connection** to check the details work.
5. If the test passes, click **Save**, then turn the toggle **on** to go live.

**Important safety notes:**
- Your secrets (passwords, tokens) are **encrypted** and are **never shown again** after you save them — you'll only see dots like `••••1234`.
- You **cannot** turn an integration on until all required details are filled in.
- If you change a detail, the CRM asks you to **test again** before going live.
- Until you connect your own accounts, the CRM stays in safe demo mode.

> Tip: Get your credentials ready with your IT person before starting. You'll paste them **into the CRM form**, never into a chat or email.

## 8. Everyday tips for each team

**Sales (BDE / BDM / Sales Head)**
- Start your day on the **Dashboard** and **Call List**.
- Use the **Pipeline** board to move deals forward by dragging cards.
- Log every call and site visit — the CRM tracks your SLAs automatically.

**Accounts (Head / Support)**
- Live in **Collections** and **Demand Letters**.
- Verify and reconcile payments; the system flags what's outstanding and overdue.

**Legal (Head / Support)**
- Manage agreements and documents through the Legal area once a booking is confirmed.

**CRM (Head / Support)**
- Handle post-sales customer care, welcome documents and ongoing communication.

**Process Admin**
- Add users, set up projects, build automation flows, and connect integrations.

**Channel Partners**
- Log in to **My Portal** to see your referrals, bookings and commission earnings.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

**Q: I can't see a menu that a colleague has.**
A: Menus depend on your role. If you need access, ask your Admin to adjust your permissions in *Roles & Access*.

**Q: Are my messages really being sent?**
A: Only if your Admin has connected the real WhatsApp/Email/Calling accounts under *Integrations*. Otherwise the CRM is in safe demo mode.

**Q: Can partners see other people's leads?**
A: No. Channel Partners are completely isolated and only ever see their own portal.

**Q: How do I find a specific lead fast?**
A: Press **Ctrl/Cmd + K** and type their name.

**Q: I made a mistake on a booking.**
A: Booking records are locked for safety. Ask your Admin/Accounts Head — every change is recorded in the Audit Log.
